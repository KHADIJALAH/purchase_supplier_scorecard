Purchase Supplier Scorecard

Score your vendors on real performance, not gut feeling

Automatic scoring

Every vendor is scored on two objective, transaction-based criteria: on-time delivery of incoming receipts and invoice accuracy against the purchase order. Both scores combine into a configurable global score and an A / B / C / Not Evaluated ranking, visible directly on the vendor's contact form.

Manual periodic evaluation

Buyers can complement the automatic score with a periodic evaluation covering quality, responsiveness and price competitiveness. Each evaluation follows a simple draft, validated, closed workflow, with a single validated evaluation allowed per vendor and per period.

Key features

  • On-time delivery score computed from confirmed incoming receipts.
  • Invoice accuracy score computed from vendor bills matched against their purchase orders.
  • Configurable weighted global score, set from Purchase Settings.
  • Automatic A / B / C / Not Evaluated ranking.
  • Manual evaluation with a draft, validated, closed workflow.
  • Filters and groupings by ranking and by evaluation period.
  • Graph view of manual global notes.
  • Dedicated security group and multi-company record rules.
  • Nightly scheduled recomputation of scores.
